This is an approximation of your MENU photo. The MMI draws the ring in 3D with its own icons and fonts, which are on the unit and not in the backup, and the 3D engine decides where the extra tile sits on the ring.
  The custom screen uses the patch's real widget positions (800 × 480): title at 13, 16, buttons from y = 100 every 70 px, status line at y = 330.
  GEM and the red menu are only placeholders here; on the car they open exactly as with SETUP + CAR and SETUP + BACK.
